Animated films, made entirely by code.

cinecode is a Claude Code plugin. Describe a film, and Claude writes it, stages every shot, animates it, scores it, mixes it and renders it. No drawing tools, no video model, no image files: a program paints every frame.

Install

In Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add zakariaf/cinecode
/plugin install cinecode@cinecode

Then open Claude Code in any folder and ask for a film in plain words, or call the skill by name:

/cinecode:cinecode make a two-minute animated film about a fox who is afraid of the dark

The first run checks Node 20+, ffmpeg and the drawing library. Voices need a GEMINI_API_KEY; everything else runs offline.

How a film is made

  1. Write. A screenplay: logline, characters, places, music, then scenes and shots with their lines.
  2. Storyboard. film.json: the cast, the sets, and every shot's camera and beats, checked field by field.
  3. Voice. Each line from Gemini TTS, transcribed back and retried until it says the words. Cached on disk.
  4. Review. Contact sheets of every shot, and a lint for staging and editing: feet, framing, eyelines, cuts.
  5. Score and mix. A procedural score with a motif, synthesized effects and ambience, mastered to −16 LUFS.
  6. Render. Full HD in parallel worker processes, with subtitles.

What's in the engine

14 character rigs

Humans of every age, and a hare, tortoise, fox, owl, squirrel and more, inked and cel-shaded, with poses, gaits, gestures and faces.

8 painted sets

Meadow, forest, stream, wayside, valley, hill, village and space, with parallax, props, walkable ground and eight times of day.

A camera

Shot sizes framed on whoever you name, push-ins, pans, cranes and moves through depth, with lead room and depth of field.

Music and sound

A score in ten moods, 39 synthesized effects, footsteps by surface, ambience beds and a mastered mix.

Effects

Rain, fireflies, mist, sparkles, and custom effects for skies and space.

Builds what is missing

No rig for a whale? No set for a city? The plugin builds the character, place or effect, and a gate checks it.
