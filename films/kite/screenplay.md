# The Kite

## Logline

On a windy hill, a little girl wants her kite to fly higher and higher; her grandfather warns her about the only tree,
and the wind takes the kite straight into it.

**Theme:** what you lose can still give you something. "Higher!" comes back at the end, said to the tree that now
flies the kite.

## Characters

### June, a girl of six (lead)
- **Rig:** `human`
- **Look:** `age` child, `hairStyle` ponytail, hair `#5a3a22`, skin `#f0c8a0`, `bottom` dress, dress `#e9b23c`
  (sunflower yellow), red scarf `#c8443a` (the kite's red), shoes `#7a3a2a`.
- **Personality:** all lift and no brakes; she wants the kite higher than anything. Bright when it flies, small when
  it's caught, and quick to laugh again.
- **Voice:** `Leda`, tone "small, high" (+ a line tone each).

### Grandpa, her grandfather
- **Rig:** `human`
- **Look:** `age` elder, `build` slim, hair `#d8d4cc`, short white beard, `top` cardigan in moss `#6b7a4a`, trousers
  `#5a4a3a`, cap `#7a6a52`, glasses.
- **Personality:** slow, warm, amused; he has seen kites lost before and doesn't mind.
- **Voice:** `Algenib`, tone "old, warm".

No narrator: the film is short enough to play on pictures and four lines.

**The kite** has no rig. It is a hidden hovering `butterfly` (cast `kite`, faded to opacity 0) that the camera can
frame and that `fly` beats move through the air; the film's `custom.mjs` overlay `kite` draws a red-and-yellow diamond
kite with a bowed tail at its position, and the string from June's hand to it.

## Screenplay

**Conventions**
- Every spoken line is captioned.
- A line is `NAME (delivery): "Text."`. The delivery becomes the line's tone.
- `(pad 0.6 s)` is a pause.

### Scene 1: The hilltop, a windy afternoon (0:02–0:28)

**Starting places:** Grandpa at `stone_right` (x 2560, just right of the standing stone) facing right, hands on hips;
June at `x:2720` on the ridge facing right, arm out with the kite string; the kite high over the slope beyond her,
left of the hawthorn (about 300 units above `beyond`).

**1.1** establishing, push-in. The whole hill under a big windy sky: the standing stone, the lone hawthorn on the
right, two small figures on the crest, and a red kite high above them, tail snaking. Wind; leaves blow across.

**1.2** full on June, low angle, static. Arm out, string climbing out of frame; she bounces with a jump for joy.
JUNE (delighted): "Higher, Grandpa! Higher!"

**1.3** medium on Grandpa, static. He looks up at the kite, smiling, then points ahead at the tree.
GRANDPA (amused): "Mind that tree, now."

**1.4** wide on June, the kite and the hawthorn, static. A gust: the kite swoops, dives, and snags in the crown of the
hawthorn with a rustle. June gasps.

**1.5** close on June. The string slack in her hand.
JUNE (sad, small): "It's stuck."

**1.6** full two-shot, static. Grandpa walks up beside her; they both sit down in the grass, looking at the tree.
GRANDPA (gentle): "Well. Now the tree is flying it."

**1.7** wide on the two of them and the tree, pull-out. The kite flutters in the hawthorn like a flag. June looks up,
throws her arm up and laughs.
JUNE (giggling): "Higher, tree!"
(hold 1.5 s)

## Ending and final image

The two of them sitting on the hilltop, small under the sky, and the red kite still dancing in the only tree. The
opening's "Higher!" was June wanting the kite away from her; now she says it to the tree, and the kite is still
flying, just not hers.
