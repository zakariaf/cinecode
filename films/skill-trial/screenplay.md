# A Light for Tilly

## Logline

Caught in the forest after dark, a little mouse is too frightened to find her way home, until a firefly lights the
path.

**Theme:** the dark is less scary with a friend's light. The returning image is the dark forest: at the start it is a
black wall around one tiny mouse; at the end she looks out at it from her den and it is full of little lights.

## Characters

### Tilly, a field mouse (lead)
- **Rig:** `mouse`
- **Look:** warm brown fur `#a08670`, cream belly `#efe4d0`; cast `scale` 1.2 (a storybook mouse, still tiny).
- **Personality:** small and cautious; freezes when scared, scurries when happy. She wants to get home to her den
  under the old oak; the dark stops her. She ends brave enough to wave at the night.
- **Voice:** `Leda`, tone "small, high"

### Glim, a firefly
- **Rig:** `firefly` (custom, in `custom.mjs`: no firefly in the cast, and the butterfly reads as a butterfly). A
  small dark beetle with a red-orange head shield, buzzing translucent wings and a pale yellow lantern tail that
  glows (a glow overlay on the `grade` layer so the night grade doesn't grey it). Always hovering about a mouse's
  head height above the ground.
- **Look:** cast `scale` 1.4.
- **Personality:** cheerful, quick, unafraid of anything; talks like the dark is the best part of the day.
- **Voice:** `Puck`, tone "bright, kind"

No narrator: the film is short and plays without one.

## Screenplay

**Conventions**
- Every spoken line is captioned.
- Shot ids are `<scene>.<shot>`.

### Scene 1: the old forest, night (0:00–0:23)

**Starting places:** Tilly sitting up on `log_top` facing left (toward home, the oak); Glim offstage right.

**1.1** establishing, push-in. The forest at night: huge dark trunks, the path, a tiny mouse on the fallen log. Title
"A Light for Tilly" in the sky. An owl hoots; Tilly flinches (gasp gesture).

**1.2** close on Tilly, static. Scared, ears up, looking left toward the dark path.
TILLY (small, trembling): "It's too dark. I can't see home."

**1.3** medium on Tilly and Glim, static. A warm glow comes in from the right: Glim flies in and hovers beside her.
Tilly turns to him. Magic sting.
GLIM (cheerful): "Need a light? Follow me!"

**1.4** close on Tilly, static. Her face changes: scared to happy. She nods.

**1.5** wide, track. Glim flies left along the path toward the oak; Tilly hops down off the log and scurries after him,
in his pool of light.

**1.6** full on Tilly and Glim at the oak, static. Tilly stops at the den under the roots, turns back to Glim.
TILLY (warm, grateful): "Thank you, Glim!"

**1.7** wide on the oak, pull-out. Tilly is in her den, peeking out. Glim rises away; around them the
forest fills with fireflies. Tilly waves at the night.

## Ending and final image

Tilly peeking out of her den under the oak, waving at a dark forest full of tiny lights. It answers the opening: the
same dark that stranded her on the log is now something she waves at.
